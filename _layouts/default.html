Hi, I'm Gagan!

I'm a Robotics and ML researcher. I'm currently a Founding Research Scientist at Dexmate, where I work on world models and foundation models for dexterous manipulation, with a focus on learning from large-scale human and robot data. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Boston Dynamics, where I worked on dexterous manipulation for the Atlas humanoid, including fine-tuning pre-trained policies with offline RL, building on my research in reinforcement learning, tactile sensing, and sim-to-real transfer.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Columbia University, where I was advised by Matei Ciocarlie. My research focused on multi-fingered dexterous manipulation, developing reinforcement learning methods that combine structured exploration, sampling-based planning, GPU-parallelized simulation, and tactile sensing to learn complex manipulation skills and transfer them to real robots. During my Ph.D., I also worked with Amazon Robotics AI and Meta Reality Labs (CTRL Labs).

Today, I'm particularly interested in learning dexterous behavior from human experience. I believe human videos and demonstrations contain rich information that can be distilled through predictive representation learning and world models into reusable priors for embodied learning. Ultimately, I want to build scalable systems that combine human experience, simulation, and real-world interaction to enable robots to learn and adapt complex skills in the real world.

I enjoy discussing research ideas, learning from others, and scheming about what's next in robotics and intelligent systems. If you're working on related problems—or just like thinking about robots that can actually use their hands—feel free to reach out.
